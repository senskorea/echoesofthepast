# Postcard collection source

Imported on 22 September 2026 at the project owner's request from
https://small.academy/eop/.

The catalogue in `public/eop-postcards.json` contains 32 historical postcards,
with the source titles, descriptions, coordinates and full article text (as
structured headings and paragraphs in `sourceContent`), plus a `detailUrl` for
each original record. The 44 `*-source-*` images are unmodified copies of images
from those records. Historical claims and locations reflect the source catalogue
and have not been independently authenticated.

Two entries were excluded: Goldfish (a non-historical image with placeholder 0,0
coordinates) and the untitled WORLD/REGION record (missing coordinates and broken
source metadata). Existing IDs for the five previously imported postcards are
preserved so saved creations and links continue to work. Older image files are
retained for locally saved postcard records that still reference them.

This is a snapshot, not an automatic synchronisation. Image and content rights
remain with their respective owners; this provenance note grants no new licence.
